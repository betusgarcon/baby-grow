12-Month Development & Sleep

At 12 months, many babies are transitioning from two naps to one. Increased gross motor activity, like standing or beginning to walk, often causes "motor leaps" that can disrupt nighttime sleep. Maintaining a consistent pre-sleep ritual remains the core key to stability during this phase.
